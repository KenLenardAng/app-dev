# FAVORITE SERIES LIST

***Hey!***
Here, I am going to list my **TOP 3** favorite series I've watched or read throughout the years in no particular order.

---

**1. SSS-Class Suicide Hunter / SSS-Class Revival Hunter (MANHWA/COMIC)**
> Its name, despite being cliche, ended up being the most unique out of all the tower manhwas I've read. It started off as generic but as the story progresses, so does the quality of the story. The way they tackle emotions and the way how the MC resolves every problem is so awesome. Be warned, this series will lead you tearful every time. I am afraid for its anime adaptation though, seeing the quality of the trailer. ***10/10 read.***

**2. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (MANGA/ANIME)**
> Classic Isekai/Otherworld series, where a person from Earth gets sent into another world. I loved the premise of him being "Reincarnated as a Slime" and it just being your average medieval action isekai fantasy (Well, I know the series is far from average but you know what I mean). I have nothing else to say, but it is genuinely a fun watch and what got me to watch series of similar genre. The series might have its faults and what others wouldn't consider "peak", but it is MY peak. ***10/10 read.***

**3. Pick Me Up, Infinite Gacha (MANHWA/COMIC)**
> I'll be honest, it took me a long time to pick this up because I thought the story would be *really* bad because of the whacky name. Became my top 3 though. I don't know what else to say but do not be emotionally attached to the characters, just a tip. ***10/10 read.***
